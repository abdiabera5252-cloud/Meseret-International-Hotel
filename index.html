Welcome to Meseret Hotel

A Beacon of Sophistication in Hawassa, Ethiopia

Recognized by its iconic multi-story cylindrical glass facade and distinctive rooftop canopy, Meseret International Hotel sets the standard for contemporary hospitality in Sidama Region.

Whether visiting for business, leisure, or special events, our guests enjoy refined comfort, modern amenities, vibrant rooftop lounge atmosphere, and direct access to the main avenues of Hawassa.
